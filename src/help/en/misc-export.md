### 1. Exporting the Question Bank
Sometimes, rather than reviewing the question bank in this extension app's question bank workspace or in an Excel or LibreOffice spreadsheet, it is more convenient to print it on paper and read it, or view it in a text editor or MS Word. In such cases, saving the question bank as a docx, txt, or pdf file makes it more convenient to review. It is also useful for distributing the question bank.

- 1.1. **Export with Answer Sheet:** If you export the question bank with an answer sheet for review purposes, you can examine not only the questions but also the correct answers.

- 1.2. **Export without Answer Sheet:** When students lack motivation to study, releasing the question bank without the answer sheet and informing them that exam questions will be randomly selected from it can encourage them to study by finding the answers on their own.

- 1.3. **Questions belonging to the same group** are indicated in the format {=n} after the question number, where n is the number of the first question in that group.
